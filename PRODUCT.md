# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Indian household grocery buyers (25–45), primarily women who cook daily. Using a mobile phone — often in a supermarket aisle, kirana store, or at home in the kitchen. OTP-literate, familiar with lucky draws. Skeptical of surveys but curious about prizes. Low friction tolerance: if a screen feels like a form, they bounce.

## Product Purpose

A gamified survey microsite that captures consumer research for Badshah Masala while creating a feel-good brand moment. Success = high completion rate, positive brand recall, and willingness to share the link. The prize draw is the hook; the warmth is the memory.

## Brand Personality

Warm, homely, authentic. Like a trusted family brand that has been in Indian kitchens for generations — not a startup, not a corporate FMCG machine. Voice: direct, warm, slightly playful. The friendly aunt who makes the best dal makhani and genuinely wants you to win.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS / Typeform minimalism (cold, de-branded, cream-on-white)
- Corporate FMCG survey template (Nielsen-style dullness, no personality)
- Startup landing page with warm-neutral parchment/cream AI defaults
- Overloaded festive clipart (2010-era marigold/rangoli collateral)

## Design Principles

1. **Feel like home, not a form** — every screen should feel like a welcome, not an interrogation.
2. **Warmth lives in depth** — rich, dark colour grounds the brand; gold and crimson carry the energy. Warmth is not cream.
3. **Earn the prize anticipation** — urgency, participation momentum, and the reveal must carry real emotional weight.
4. **One committed colour, owned** — crimson is the voice. Don't hedge with neutrals around the edges.
5. **Mobile-first, thumb-first** — designed for one-handed use while standing in a grocery aisle.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. All body text ≥4.5:1 contrast. Prefers-reduced-motion respected throughout. Touch targets minimum 44×44pt. Numeric keyboard on all number inputs.
